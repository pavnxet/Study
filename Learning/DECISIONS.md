# Architectural Decisions (`DECISIONS.md`)

This file is a running log of meaningful decisions made while writing or changing code in this project. It exists so human reviewers and future agent sessions understand *why* the code looks the way it does.

## Rules for Maintaining This File
- **Log in real-time**: Record entries during the turn the choice is made — never retroactively.
- **Meaningful decisions only**: Choice of approach, choice of library/tool, rejected alternatives, data model/schema design, performance vs simplicity trade-offs. Skip trivial edits (typos, renames, formatting).
- **Format**: Newest entries go at the top.

---

## Entry Template

### [YYYY-MM-DD] Short Title of Decision

**What changed:** One or two sentences describing the implementation change.

**Why this approach:** The reasoning — what problem this solves and why this design over alternatives.

**Why this library/tool (if applicable):** Why this specific dependency was chosen over alternatives, or why custom logic was written instead.

**Alternatives considered:** What other options were on the table and why they were rejected.

**Trade-offs & limitations:** What this decision costs (complexity, performance, flexibility).
